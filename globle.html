    How to play

    Each game picks one of 197 countries. Guess a country and it fills on the map — white is far, red is close.

    • The number by each guess is kilometres between the two countries’ closest borders.
    • 0 km means they share a land border.
    • France is metropolitan France, so French Guiana does not count as a France–Brazil border.
    • Pinch or scroll to zoom. Drag to pan.
